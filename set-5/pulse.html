FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is FixPapa Pulse?

FixPapa Pulse is an intelligent device health and lifecycle management platform designed to help businesses monitor, verify, diagnose, and proactively manage the health of their IT devices.

It can provide device health information, hardware diagnostics, health reports, historical trends, alerts and predictive insights, depending on the selected plan and supported device/platform.

Which devices can FixPapa Pulse monitor?

FixPapa Pulse is designed to support business IT devices such as:

  • Laptops
  • Desktops
  • Workstations
  • Servers
  • Other compatible computing devices

Actual features and availability may vary depending on the operating system, hardware configuration, device permissions and subscription plan.

Which operating systems are supported?

FixPapa Pulse supports currently supported and generally available operating-system versions for which FixPapa Pulse has released compatibility.

Supported platforms may include:

  • Microsoft Windows
  • Apple macOS

Support is subject to the specific version, device, architecture, hardware configuration and technical requirements published by FixPapa.

Does FixPapa Pulse support Windows XP or Windows 7?

No. FixPapa Pulse does not guarantee compatibility with obsolete or end-of-life Windows versions such as Windows XP, Windows 7, or any other Windows version that is no longer officially supported by Microsoft, unless FixPapa specifically announces support for that version.

The same principle applies to other operating systems.

What does “supported operating system” mean?

A supported operating system means a version that:

Is officially supported by its manufacturer/developer, and

Meets FixPapa Pulse's technical and security requirements, and

Is specifically compatible with the applicable FixPapa Pulse application/agent.

FixPapa may discontinue support for an operating-system version when it becomes obsolete, reaches end-of-support, or is no longer technically or securely compatible.

Does FixPapa Pulse support macOS?

Yes, supported and compatible macOS versions may be supported.

FixPapa Pulse may not support obsolete macOS releases that are no longer supported by Apple or that do not meet the technical requirements of the FixPapa Pulse application.

Compatibility may also depend on the Mac hardware architecture and other technical requirements.

Will FixPapa Pulse automatically support every new OS version?

Not necessarily.

A newly released operating-system version may require compatibility testing, application updates, API changes or security validation.

FixPapa will make commercially reasonable efforts to support new generally available OS versions, but compatibility is not guaranteed immediately upon release.

What happens when an operating system reaches End-of-Life?

FixPapa may discontinue support for that OS version.

Customers may be required to upgrade the device operating system to a supported version to continue using FixPapa Pulse features.

Can FixPapa Pulse work without an internet connection?

Some diagnostic or verification functions may operate locally depending on the product configuration.

However, features such as:

  • Cloud records
  • Dashboard synchronization
  • Monitoring
  • Historical trends
  • Email alerts
  • Predictive alerts
  • Centralized reporting

may require an active internet connection.

Does FixPapa Pulse require administrator permission?

Certain diagnostics, hardware information and monitoring functions may require appropriate system permissions or administrator privileges.

If required permissions are not provided, certain features may be unavailable or provide limited information.

What does FixPapa Pulse check?

Depending on the plan and platform, FixPapa Pulse may evaluate areas such as:

  • Hardware health
  • Performance
  • Storage
  • Battery health
  • System information
  • Hardware components
  • Device configuration
  • Security-related parameters
  • Historical health trends

The exact tests and features may vary by operating system, device and subscription.

Can FixPapa Pulse predict hardware failure?

FixPapa Pulse may use available device-health data, historical information and diagnostic indicators to identify potential risk or early warning signals.

However, predictive alerts are risk indicators and not a guarantee that a device will fail or will not fail.

Can FixPapa Pulse tell me whether I should repair or replace a device?

FixPapa Pulse can provide device health and diagnostic information that may assist businesses in making repair, maintenance or replacement decisions.

Such recommendations should be treated as decision-support information rather than an absolute guarantee of the economic or technical outcome.

Can I generate a device health report?

Yes. Depending on your plan and supported platform, FixPapa Pulse can generate device health and diagnostic reports.

What is the Verify plan?

The Verify plan is intended for users who need a one-time or short-duration assessment of a device.

Features may include hardware testing, QR certification, cloud records and health reports, subject to the applicable plan.

What is the Monitor plan?

The Monitor plan is designed for ongoing device health monitoring and may include:

  • Unlimited scans
  • Automated health checks
  • Predictive alerts
  • Historical trends
  • Email alerts
  • Health-score evolution
  • Battery-health trends
  • Storage-health trends

Features are subject to platform compatibility and the applicable subscription.

Does FixPapa Pulse support enterprise deployments?

Yes. Enterprise customers may receive additional capabilities such as:

  • Dashboard integration
  • API access
  • Customization
  • Enterprise reporting
  • Priority support
  • Dedicated account management

Enterprise functionality is subject to the agreed commercial and technical scope.

Can FixPapa Pulse integrate with our existing systems?

Enterprise customers may be able to integrate FixPapa Pulse through available APIs or other agreed integration mechanisms.

Integration scope, availability and pricing will depend on the technical requirements and commercial agreement.

Is FixPapa Pulse suitable for personal devices?

FixPapa Pulse may be used for individual devices where the applicable plan permits it. However, the platform is primarily designed for business and organizational IT infrastructure management.

Does FixPapa guarantee 100% accuracy of device-health results?

No.

Device-health results depend on available hardware information, operating-system APIs, permissions, sensors, device configuration and other technical factors.

FixPapa Pulse provides diagnostic and decision-support information, and results should not be interpreted as an absolute guarantee of device condition.

Can FixPapa Pulse detect every hardware problem?

No.

Certain hardware failures may not be detectable through software-based diagnostics, particularly intermittent, physical, mechanical or component-level faults that cannot be accessed through available system interfaces.

For such cases, physical inspection by a qualified technician may be required.

What happens if my device is not compatible?

FixPapa Pulse may display limited functionality or may not be installable/usable on unsupported devices or operating systems.

FixPapa may provide a compatibility list or technical requirements from time to time.

Will FixPapa Pulse slow down my computer?

FixPapa Pulse is designed to operate efficiently. However, resource usage may vary depending on device configuration, operating system, scanning activity and monitoring frequency.

How secure is my device information?

FixPapa takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed through FixPapa Pulse.

Customers should also maintain appropriate device security, operating-system updates, access controls and network security.

Will FixPapa sell my device data?

FixPapa should not sell or commercially disclose customer-confidential device information except as permitted under the applicable agreement, privacy policy or with the customer's authorization.

Can I use FixPapa Pulse on multiple devices?

Yes, subject to the applicable subscription, license limits and commercial terms.

Enterprise plans may offer customized device volumes and licensing.
