What’s covered, and what’s not

Covered

  • Hardware parts that stop working in normal use
  • Motherboard, RAM, storage, display, keyboard
  • Power supply, charging port and adapter faults
  • Parts and labour, up to your plan limit

Not covered

  • Drops, cracks, liquid or burn damage
  • Software, viruses and data loss
  • Scratches, dents and normal wear
  • Faults that existed before the plan

Who can buy

  • Device is under 5 years old
  • It works when the plan starts (no known faults)
  • Original or certified parts inside
  • Passes a quick health check at pickup or on video

FAQs

Good to know

Anything else? Call +91 86965 44448 or write to us.

Can I buy it if my brand warranty has ended?

Yes. That’s exactly what it’s for. Your device needs to be in working condition and pass a quick health check before cover starts.

When does my cover start?

Cover starts after the health check. Claims open after a short waiting period on the 3 and 6 month plans, shown on each plan.

What happens if the repair costs more than my plan limit?

We cover up to your plan limit and share the balance with you before doing any work. You decide whether to go ahead.

Is accidental or liquid damage covered?

No. This plan covers hardware that fails in normal use. Damage from drops, liquid or burns is not included.

Can I transfer the warranty if I sell my device?

Yes. Contact our support team with the new owner’s details and we’ll move the cover across.

Not sure if your device qualifies?

Share the model and age. We’ll tell you in minutes.
